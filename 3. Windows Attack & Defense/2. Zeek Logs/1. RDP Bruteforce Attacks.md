## Description

The concept of an RDP brute force attack is relatively straightforward: attackers attempt to login into a Remote Desktop session by systematically guessing and trying different passwords until they find the correct one.

## Detecting RDP Brute Force Attacks With Splunk & Zeek Logs

Now let's explore how we can identify RDP brute force attacks, using Splunk and Zeek logs.

```shell-session
index="rdp_bruteforce" sourcetype="bro:rdp:json"
| bin _time span=5m
| stats count values(cookie) by _time, id.orig_h, id.resp_h
| where count>30
```
